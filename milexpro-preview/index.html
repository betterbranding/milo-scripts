01

The product

What is MILEX™?

MILEX™ is the parent material behind every MILEXPRO™ product: an all-natural insulation material made from domestically grown grain sorghum.

The process begins when farmer-growers deliver naturally grown, non-GMO sorghum seeds to the processing mill in Tulia, Texas. Those seeds move through a patented manufacturing process, then are extruded at high speed with more than 3,000 psi to create MILEX™'s signature Thermal Puff™ technology.

  1. 01HarvestNon-GMO grain sorghum delivered by local farmers.
  2. 02RefineProcessed through patented state-of-the-art equipment in Tulia, Texas.
  3. 03ExtrudeHigh-speed extrusion at 3,000+ psi creates Thermal Puffs™.
02

Patented Thermal Puff™ technology

It's all in the shape

Each Thermal Puff™ is elongated and curved, roughly two to three times longer than wide, with rounded ends and a porous, spongy surface. That interlocking shape is what lets MILEX™ build a continuous, gap-free thermal blanket in the attic.

The process behind it is backed by seven process patents, covering everything from the sorghum refinement to the high-pressure extrusion that forms every puff.

03

Safe & clean

Naturally pest-free

During processing, all fats and sugars are removed, rendering MILEX™ a non-food source.

  • Pests are naturally not attracted to it.
  • Cured with a low-percentage boric powder finish (under 1%) that also adds pest resistance.
  • MILEX™ self-extinguishes and will not sustain combustion.
  • Free of potentially harmful polymers. Virtually dust-free, non-toxic, non-irritant.

The science

Physics, not chemicals.

How MILEX™ Thermal MAX® builds a continuous thermal barrier in the attic, engineered to meet the spec on every job.

Submit Your Plans→
01

Heat never sleeps

On a hot August afternoon, an unconditioned attic can climb past 140 degrees

That heat sits a few inches above the ceiling, radiating down while the HVAC system fights it around the clock. Heat always moves toward cold, and it only travels three ways. Stop all three, and the home holds its temperature. That's exactly what a MILEX™ thermal barrier is engineered to do.

↓

Conduction

Heat passing through solid material. Air is one of the worst heat conductors in nature, and every Thermal Puff™ traps thousands of microscopic pockets of it, so heat has almost nothing solid to travel through.

↺

Convection

Heat carried by moving air. Loose, gappy insulation lets attic air wash heat straight into the ceiling. MILEX™ puffs interlock into a continuous, gap-free blanket that shuts the air currents down.

☀

Radiation

Heat traveling as invisible waves from the roof deck. A deep MILEX™ layer at R-3.25 per inch absorbs and slows radiant heat before it reaches the drywall.

R-values tested per ASTM C518. Actual performance varies by installation.

03

The thermal barrier effect

One barrier. Both seasons.

Insulation doesn't make heat or cold. It slows the exchange between inside and outside. A deep, continuous MILEX™ barrier works both directions, all year.

Winter

Holds conditioned heat in place

Warm air rises, and in an under-insulated attic it escapes straight through the ceiling. A MILEX™ barrier traps that rising heat inside the living space, so rooms hold their warmth and the furnace gets to rest.

Summer

Slows the heat pressing down

The roof deck soaks up sun all day and turns the attic into an oven. The barrier slows that heat before it presses down through the ceiling, so the AC system isn't fighting a losing battle.

≈

Even, steady comfort

Room-to-room temperatures level out and stay there, all year.

↘

Shorter HVAC cycles

When the barrier holds conditioned air in place, the system runs less and wears less.

04

The clean-air science

Easy on the air, easy on the crew

Most insulation problems on a jobsite are really air quality problems. Fiberglass sheds microscopic glass particles, and chemical binders in some legacy loose-fill products off-gas for years. MILEX™ takes a different path: inert, all-natural Thermal Puffs™ with nothing added that doesn't belong in the air.

  • No glass particles to become airborne and cycle through the HVAC system
  • No formaldehyde-based binders or off-gassing adhesives
  • Fats and sugars removed during processing, so it's not a food source for pests
  • Finished with a light dusting of naturally occurring boric powder to help deter pests

The result is virtually dust-free, non-toxic, non-irritant insulation your install crews and your buyers will both notice.
