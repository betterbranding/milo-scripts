The journey

Grown on the high plains

It begins in a Texas sorghum field. Our farmer-growers deliver naturally grown, non-GMO sorghum seeds, or "berries", to our processing mill in Tulia, Texas. A renewable agricultural crop, grown by local farmers, supporting American agriculture.

02 / 04

Refined to the fiber

These tiny sorghum berries are passed through state-of-the-art equipment in our patented manufacturing process. All fats and sugars are removed, rendering MILEX™ a non-food source that pests are naturally not attracted to.

03 / 04

Engineered into Thermal Puffs

Once refined, the berries are extruded at high speed with more than 3000 psi, creating our amazing "Thermal Puffs". Each puff contains thousands of tiny air pockets that create an incredible thermal blanket.

The product

What is MILEX™ Thermal MAX®?

Nature's alternative. MILEX™ is made from a special type of domestically grown Sorghum Vulgare, commonly referred to as "Milo".

The process begins when our farmer-growers deliver naturally grown, non-GMO sorghum seeds, or "berries", to our processing mill in Tulia, Texas. These tiny sorghum berries undergo our patented manufacturing process where they are passed through state-of-the-art equipment. Once refined, they are extruded at high speed with more than 3000 psi, creating our amazing "Thermal Puffs": MILEX™.

03

Sustainability

Why use sorghum?

Sorghum makes the perfect insulation because it expands naturally without the use of expensive, potentially hazardous polymers, making it safer, better and far less expensive than other expandable materials.

It has a much lower carbon footprint than other grains. Sorghum is known as one of the most water efficient crops grown, largely due to its drought tolerance properties, and is recognized as one of the most efficient crops in converting solar energy and water use.

  • Drought tolerant
  • Low carbon footprint
  • Solar efficient
  • Biodegradable to ISO standards
