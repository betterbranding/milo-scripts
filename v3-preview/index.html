Sustainability

Why use sorghum?

Sorghum makes the perfect insulation because it expands naturally without the use of expensive, potentially hazardous polymers, making it a safer, more sustainable choice than expandable materials that rely on those polymers.

It has a much lower carbon footprint than other grains. Sorghum is known as one of the most water efficient crops grown, largely due to its drought tolerance properties, and is recognized as one of the most efficient crops in converting solar energy and water use.

How MILEX™ Thermal MAX® builds a thermal barrier in your attic that keeps winter heat in, summer heat out, and your electric bill down.

01

Heat never sleeps

On a hot August afternoon, your attic can hit 140+ degrees

All that heat sits a few inches above your ceiling, radiating down into your living room while your AC fights it around the clock. You feel it as a stuffy upstairs and a thermostat that never seems satisfied. Your utility company sees it as revenue.

Here's what most people never learn: heat always moves toward cold, and it only has three ways to travel. Stop all three, and your home holds its temperature. That's exactly what a MILEX™ thermal barrier does.

↓

Conduction

Heat passing through solid material, like a pan handle getting hot. Air is one of the worst heat conductors in nature, and every Thermal Puff™ traps thousands of microscopic pockets of it. Heat has almost nothing solid to travel through.

↺

Convection

Heat carried by moving air. Loose, gappy insulation lets attic air wash heat straight into your ceiling. MILEX™ puffs interlock into a continuous, gap-free blanket that shuts the air currents down.

☀

Radiation

Heat traveling as invisible waves from your roof deck. A deep MILEX™ layer at R-3.25 per inch absorbs and slows radiant heat before it ever reaches your drywall.

R-values tested per ASTM C518. Actual performance varies by installation.

03

The thermal barrier effect

One barrier. Both seasons.

Insulation doesn't make heat or cold. It slows the exchange between inside and outside. A deep, continuous MILEX™ barrier works both directions, all year.

Winter

Keeps the heat you paid for

Warm air rises, and in an under-insulated home it escapes straight through the ceiling. The MILEX™ barrier traps that rising heat in your living space, so rooms hold their warmth and your furnace finally gets to rest.

Summer

Blocks the heat you didn't ask for

Your roof soaks up sun all day and turns your attic into an oven. The barrier stops that heat from pressing down through the ceiling, so your AC cools the house once and keeps it there.

≈

Even, steady comfort

No more cold floors in January or an upstairs you avoid in August. Room-to-room temperatures level out and stay there.

↘

Shorter HVAC cycles

When the barrier holds your conditioned air in place, your system runs less, wears less, and lasts longer.

The clean-air science

Easy on the air. Easy on your lungs.

Most insulation problems are really air quality problems. Fiberglass sheds microscopic glass particles that ride your HVAC system into every room. Chemical binders off-gas for years. Your family breathes all of it.

MILEX™ takes a different path: inert, all-natural Thermal Puffs™ with nothing added that doesn't belong in your air.

  • No glass particles to become airborne and cycle through your vents
  • No formaldehyde-based binders or off-gassing adhesives
  • Fats and sugars removed during processing, so it's not a food source for pests
  • Finished with a light dusting of naturally occurring boric powder to help deter pests

The result is virtually dust-free, non-toxic, non-irritant insulation chosen by homeowners who care about the air inside their home. Comfort you can feel, in air you can trust.
